# RELS 398 — Scholar Labs evidence on course structure

## 01. Sequencing a digital scholarly editing / TEI curriculum

**Query:** *In what sequence should a digital scholarly editing curriculum teach cataloguing, transcription, normalization, collation, TEI markup, annotation, and publication, and what evidence supports that ordering?*

**1. [The Scholarly Edition Workflow: Tradition Meets Technology](https://books.google.com/books?hl=en&lr=&id=S2G9EQAAQBAJ&oi=fnd&pg=PT29&ots=O18UvoNfue&sig=8LGG50qhN8WGvpw89J9CH-ZGrfI)** · [PDF](https://ubiquitypress.com/chapters/26/files/06cd21b5-e232-4e51-9ab8-2890986f100a.pdf)  
*KM Penner - Artificial Intelligence for Bible Translation and …, 2026 - books.google.comDescribes*  

> a structured workflow mirroring the seven-stage traditional process of creating a scholarly edition, detailing steps from manuscript identification and transcription to normalization, and culminating in a final project.

- **Course Methodology:** Details the sequence of activities in an undergraduate course, starting with manuscript identification (catalog information), proceeding to transcription and reconciliation, followed by normalizing, and ultimately presenting the final output using TEI Publisher.
- **TEI Output Sequence:** Explains that the final output of the course was a TEI conformant XML file that included a TEI header with catalog information, a diplomatic transcription, a normalized text with textual variants, lemmas, and parsings, a translation, and comments, suggesting this is the publication sequence.

**2. [Epigraphers and encoders: strategies for teaching and learning digital epigraphy](https://ubiquitypress.com/chapters/26/files/06cd21b5-e232-4e51-9ab8-2890986f100a.pdf)** · cited by 33  
*[PDF][PDF] Epigraphers and encoders: strategies for teaching and learning digital epigraphyG Bodard, S Stoyanova - Digital Classics Outside the Echo …, 2016 - ubiquitypress.comDiscusses*  

> a change in the EpiDoc training workshop sequence, which shifted from introducing detailed text markup first, then monument description markup, to starting with an overview of the entire document structure, followed by monument description and detailed text markup last.

- **Evidence for Sequencing:** Explains the evidence supporting the revised teaching strategy, noting that starting with the document structure and monument description proved clearer and more comprehensible to students, immediately demonstrating that the EpiDoc document structure mirrors that of a traditional epigraphic publication.
- **Placement of Indexing:** Describes the placement of lemmatizing and indexing at the end of the EpiDoc training, illustrating that the rigorous intellectual effort of indexing in a traditional project is changed but not replaced by an automated process in the digital workflow, which follows the logical progression of an epigraphic project.

**3. [Evaluating AI for the Digital Scholarly Edition](https://www.torrossa.com/gs/resourceProxy?an=6192637&publisher=FZ8178#page=168)**  
*KM Penner - Artificial Intelligence for Bible Translation and …, 2026 - torrossa.comDescribes*  

> a structured workflow for a course on digital methods for historical documents that mirrors a seven-stage traditional process for creating a scholarly edition, culminating in a ten-step process.

- **Course Sequence:** Outlines a ten-step sequence used in an undergraduate course, starting with transcription, followed by reconciliation, normalizing, and culminating in presentation, with the final project being a TEI conformant XML file.
- **TEI File Contents:** Explains the use of the resulting TEI XML file, which contained catalog information in the teiHeader, a diplomatic transcription, a normalized text with textual variants, lemmas, and parsings, a translation, and comments, demonstrating the integrated nature of the steps.

**4. [Digitization education](https://www.dlib.org/dlib/march09/dahlstrom/03dahlstrom.html?viewType=Print&viewClass=Print)** · [PDF](https://library.oapen.org/bitstream/handle/20.500.12657/75994/1/9781000858389.pdf#page=111) · cited by 32  
*[HTML][HTML] Digitization educationM Dahlström, A Doracic - D-Lib Magazine, 2009 - dlib.orgDescribes*  

> the curriculum of a cultural heritage digitization course that includes teaching the "whole digitization chain" from project planning to output/publishing on the web.

- **Sequence of Instruction:** Outlines the pedagogical sequence of instruction as project planning, text and image capture (scanning, OCR, or transcription), editing/management (image editing, XML text encoding, annotation, database management), and output/publishing on the web (XSL transformation).
- **Pedagogical Support:** Explains the pedagogical approach that requires students to produce different text transcription level outputs (diplomatic and normalized text) from one source by using TEI and XSLT, enhancing flexibility and reusability.

**5. [Remodeling the Text Encoding Initiative (TEI) Workshop](https://library.oapen.org/bitstream/handle/20.500.12657/75994/1/9781000858389.pdf#page=111)** · [PDF](https://univ-grenoble-alpes.hal.science/hal-01182162/document)  
*[PDF][PDF] Remodeling the Text Encoding Initiative (TEI) WorkshopJ Russell, MI Maza, LM Cenci… - Digital Humanities …, 2023 - library.oapen.orgDescribes*  

> a graduate course where students first focused on histories and theories of editing early modern plays, followed by practical matters such as establishing and mediating the playtext and editorial interventions using XML/TEI.

- **Pedagogical Rationale:** Suggests that working through data models before getting to encoding helps students articulate assumptions about the text structure, which facilitates learning the conceptual bases of digital scholarly editing.
- **Encoding Training Focus:** Explains that the initial training for graduate students included an introduction to XML and TEI Guidelines, focusing on core elements and the drama module, to enable them to complete the text encoding task.

**6. [Digital scholarly editing: Theories, models and methods](https://books.google.com/books?hl=en&lr=&id=4QnACwAAQBAJ&oi=fnd&pg=PP1&ots=p3HhK8-rzl&sig=dZXCyBSwVhRR4gGuJ_NwNuSfeiY)** · cited by 401  
*[BOOK][B] Digital scholarly editing: Theories, models and methodsE Pierazzo - 2016 - books.google.comSuggests*  

> the typical workflow of preparing an edition begins with choosing the object to edit, followed by editorial work, then post-production and publication, and finally, long-term issues.

- **Textbook Timeline:** Proposes a curriculum timeline that guides the reader along the ideal ‘textbook’ timeline of preparing an edition, starting from the elaboration of abstract models of the objects being edited.
- **Theoretical Examination:** Aims to examine the theoretical and methodological issues and problems that emerge during the stages of digital scholarly editing when applying computational techniques and methods.

**7. [Hands-on teaching digital humanities: a didactic analysis of a summer school course on digital editing](https://www.jstor.org/content/pdf/oa_chapter_edited/j.ctt5vjtt3.7)** · [PDF](https://kclpure.kcl.ac.uk/portal/files/137276639/Reassembling_Scholarly_Communications_EVE_and_GRAY_Published13October2020_GOLD_VoR_CC_BY_.pdf#page=155) · cited by 43  

> M Rehbein, C Fritze - Digital Humanities Pedagogy. Open Book Publishers …, 2012 - JSTORDiscusses the rationale behind a week-long summer school course on digital editing, including its learning objectives, content, methods, and tools.

- **Sequential Workflow:** Explains that the course used a "learning-by-project" approach where the results from one phase of editorial work built the basis for the next phase, suggesting a sequential workflow for transcription and encoding as initial steps.
- **Transcription and Encoding Definition:** Defines transcription as transforming texts into machine-readable format (sequence of characters) and encoding (using TEI P5 Guidelines) as the subsequent step of enriching this raw data with structural and semantic features.

**8. [The Making of Empirical Knowledge: Recipes, Craft, and Scholarly Communication](https://kclpure.kcl.ac.uk/portal/files/137276639/Reassembling_Scholarly_Communications_EVE_and_GRAY_Published13October2020_GOLD_VoR_CC_BY_.pdf#page=155)** · [PDF](https://journals.openedition.org/jtei/pdf/1076) · cited by 1  
*[PDF][PDF] The Making of Empirical Knowledge: Recipes, Craft, and Scholarly CommunicationPH Smith, TH Uchacz, N Rosenkranz, CC Sabel - 2020 - kclpure.kcl.ac.ukDescribes*  

> a pedagogical initiative, the Making and Knowing Project, which focused on creating a digital critical edition of a 16th-century manuscript, blending collaborative scholarship, digital humanities, and material practices.

- **Initial Encoding and Transcription:** Explains that the first stage of the digital critical edition process involved paleography workshops where graduate students gained skills by transcribing, translating, and encoding the manuscript.
- **Workflow Sequence:** Details the sequence of outcomes from the paleography workshops, which produced a finalized diplomatic transcription, a normalized transcription, and an English translation, all marked-up using a custom XML tag set derived from the Text Encoding Initiative (TEI).

**9. [Issue 12| July 2019-May 2020 Selected Papers from the 2017 TEI Conference](https://journals.openedition.org/jtei/pdf/1076)** · [PDF](https://journals.openedition.org/jtei/pdf/1804)  

> [PDF][PDF] Issue 12| July 2019-May 2020 Selected Papers from the 2017 TEI ConferenceJ Jenstad, K Tomasek, M Holmes - journals.openedition.orgDescribes a graduate seminar where students first transcribed and encoded letters to develop initial encoding guidelines.

- **Encoding Precedes Transcription:** Explains a pedagogical scenario where undergraduate research assistants start by encoding letters already transcribed by the professor before moving on to transcribing and encoding longer, more complex letters.
- **Publication as Final Step:** Notes that the TAPAS (TEI Archiving, Preservation, and Access Service) publication venue is used as an end-point after a completed text is ready to publish, supporting its role as the final stage.

**10. [The TEI Assignment in the Literature Classroom: Making a Lord Mayor's Show in University and College Classrooms](https://journals.openedition.org/jtei/1804)** · cited by 2  
*M Kaethler - Journal of the Text Encoding Initiative, 2020 - journals.openedition.orgDescribes*  

> a pedagogical sequence where students first work through lessons on textual editing, including transcription and digital facsimiles, before embarking on transcription and then text encoding using TEI.

- **Curriculum Components:** Explains that the curriculum includes lessons on textual editing and digital facsimiles, and that students are tasked with transcribing from a source and consulting a print edition before encoding the transcription.
- **Introduction to TEI:** Mentions that students were introduced to XML and TEI through a guide and a guest lecture before practicing text encoding in a specific software environment.

## 03. Teaching HTR and Transkribus to undergraduates

**Query:** *What pedagogical practices are effective when teaching handwritten text recognition and Transkribus to undergraduate students in manuscript studies courses?*

**1. [Teaching handwritten text recognition: can new technologies save old skills?](https://brill.com/view/journals/qua/54/2-3/article-p198_6.xml)** · [PDF](https://hal.science/hal-02520508v3/file/20018%20Transkribus%20TR-ENrelu_RS260620_V3.pdf) · cited by 4  
*B Caers - Quaerendo, 2024 - brill.comReports*  

> on a course taught at Leiden University, where students have been introduced to Transkribus for four years.

- **Introductory Session Skills:** Explains that students acquire the basic skills to navigate the program and produce basic transcriptions in an introductory session.
- **Theoretical Context:** Embeds the Transkribus work in a theoretical discussion about text criticism and editing, focusing on the impact of increased digital availability of primary material.

**2. [Optical Recognition Assisted Transcription with Transkribus: The Experiment concerning Eugène Wilhelm's Personal Diary (1885-1951)](https://hal.science/hal-02520508/)** · [PDF](https://researchrepository.ul.ie/bitstreams/b608ffb4-f630-4dd8-ab48-dec3d7caf437/download) · cited by 15  
*R Schlagdenhauffen - Journal of Data Mining and Digital Humanities, 2020 - hal.scienceDetails*  

> a collaborative transcription experiment conducted with students in a classroom setting, noting the difficulties encountered and the solutions implemented to address them.

- **Interface Simplicity:** Explains that the interface of Transkribus was found to be simple for students, suggesting the main difficulties were related to the handwriting itself rather than the software.
- **Greek Alphabet and Collective Reading:** Mentions that introducing students to or reminding them of the Greek alphabet was helpful, alongside collectively reading the diary pages.

**3. [Making Space](https://www.jstor.org/stable/27181293)** · [PDF](https://journals.openedition.org/jtei/pdf/2926) · cited by 3  

> C Breathnach, K Mulrennan, S Keogh - The History Teacher, 2021 - JSTORImplemented flipped classroom principles and continuous assessment, utilizing a virtual learning environment (VLE) for teaching and testing practical skills outside of class time to free up face-to-face contact time for practice-based learning (PBL).

- **Practice-Based Learning:** Employed Practice-Based Learning (PBL) via archival transcriptions of coronial court records, where students interacted with digital surrogates of archival materials in a classroom setting to gain practical experience and awareness of digital humanities tools.
- **Utilizing Transkribus:** Noted the availability of transcription software like Transkribus and FromThePage as useful aids for demonstrating reusable transcription data, though the particular study was limited in its ability to use them due to IT restrictions and data sensitivity.

**4. [Collaborative Encoding of Text Genesis: A Pedagogical Approach for Teaching Genetic Encoding with the TEI](https://journals.openedition.org/jtei/2926)** · cited by 3  
*S Apostolo, I Börner, A Hechtl - Journal of the Text …, 2020 - journals.openedition.orgDescribes*  

> a pedagogical approach where participants collaboratively produced a text using various writing tools and paper types, with the process recorded on video, to create a multi-layered source for analysis and transcription.

- **Transkribus and Text Analysis:** Introduces Transkribus for transcription after students analyzed specific phenomena (additions, deletions, corrections, scribal hands) in the digitized texts they produced, followed by export and enrichment in Oxygen Author.
- **Effective Workshop Structure:** Found a shorter workshop version successful even with a larger group and limited time, provided students had prior exposure to Transkribus and basic TEI, and multiple instructors were available for support.

**5. [A TEACHING GUIDE TO READING MARGINAL NOTES](https://library.iated.org/view/HRISTOVAYORDANOVA2022ATE)** · [PDF](https://bibliotekanauki.pl/articles/63519213.pdf)  
*K Hristova-Yordanova - EDULEARN22 Proceedings, 2022 - library.iated.orgRecommends*  

> creating specialized teaching guides written by qualified linguists with expertise in paleography or historical handwriting for major scripts like Latin, Greek, and Cyrillic to help students work with marginal notes.

- **Automating Deciphering with Software:** Suggests that students can be included in automating the deciphering process by using specially developed software, such as Transkribus, for turning handwritten text into machine-written text.
- **Digital Environment Learning:** Outlines an educational approach that includes teaching students how to recognize and read marginal notes in a digital environment, ensuring they are acquainted with special characters and how to present them using specific fonts.

**6. [Data storytelling around André Mazon's correspondence](https://www.ceeol.com/search/article-detail?id=1300418)** · [PDF](https://www.euppublishing.com/doi/pdf/10.3366/ijhac.2024.0319)  
*S Cinková, B Hladká, J Mírovský… - Colloquia Humanistica, 2024 - ceeol.comUtilizes*  

> a "data story" concept in an international one-semester data analytics course for social sciences and humanities students to embed text processing methods like Transkribus within realistic workflows.

- **Step-by-Step Transkribus Training:** Instructs students to transcribe original scans using Transkribus or Pero, following a process from uploading documents and setting up recognition to manual correction and data export (including XML format to preserve connection to original scans).
- **HTR Tool Application:** Engages students in tasks that require using handwritten text recognition (HTR) tools and machine learning concepts, such as prioritizing letters for transcription to either train an HTR model or conduct research on a prominent topic, thereby teaching foundational data literacy.

**7. [Integrating Digital Editions and Methods for Text Editing and Analysis in Undergraduate Literary Studies](https://www.euppublishing.com/doi/abs/10.3366/ijhac.2024.0319)** · [PDF](https://jdmdh.episciences.org/6736/pdf) · cited by 2  
*S Stoyanova - International Journal of Humanities and Arts …, 2024 - euppublishing.comIntroduces*  

> undergraduate students to digital text editing tools, specifically mentioning Transkribus Lite, in a course on Italian literature and digital humanities.

- **Text Editing for Close Reading:** Emphasizes that digital text editing provides a pedagogical method that creates opportunities for situated learning and encourages very close attention to textual elements.
- **Transcription Tool Exercise:** Used a tool exercise assignment where students worked with Transkribus Lite to transcribe a facsimile image from the Zibaldone, despite challenges related to language proficiency and unfamiliarity with reading handwritten Italian.

[Transcription assistée par reconnaissance optique avec Transkribus: L'expérience du journal intime d'Eugène Wilhelm (1885-1951)](https://jdmdh.episciences.org/6736/pdf)** · [PDF](https://repository.arizona.edu/bitstream/handle/10150/672973/Transcribing-Recipe-Manuscripts-EMSJ-2022.pdf?sequence=1)  

> [PDF][PDF] Transcription assistée par reconnaissance optique avec

- **Transkribus:** L'expérience du journal intime d'Eugène Wilhelm (1885-1951)R Schlagdenhauffen - Journal of Data Mining & Digital …, 2020 - jdmdh.episciences.orgDetails a collaborative transcription experiment conducted with Master students in a classroom setting, outlining the observed difficulties and the solutions found to address them.Interface vs.
- **Handwriting Difficulty:** Found that students did not face particular difficulty with the Transkribus interface itself, but rather with the challenges presented by the singular nature of the diarist's handwriting.
- **Effective Software Features:** Suggests that the Transkribus OCR feature, which allows for storing scans and transcripts in a shareable collection, is particularly useful for teaching.

**9. [Transcribing Recipe Manuscripts Online: Vb 380 and the “What's in a Recipe?” Undergraduate Research Project at Penn State Abington](https://repository.arizona.edu/handle/10150/672973)** · [PDF](https://e-archivo.uc3m.es/bitstreams/240da76b-4b0e-4003-bb17-5297e9d00138/download) · cited by 1  
*H Froehlich, M Nicosia, C Riehman-Murphy - 2022 - repository.arizona.eduDiscusses*

- **Legitimate Peripheral Participation:** Presents a legitimate peripheral participation framework for students using large-scale digital projects to foster their identities as researchers in scholarly communities of practice.
- **Pedagogical Practices:** Explains that pedagogical practices include sustained focus on a single digitized early modern manuscript recipe book, multimodal engagement, and connecting students with the EMROC scholarly community.

**10. [Training eyes and training hands in the digital research with manuscripts](https://e-archivo.uc3m.es/bitstreams/240da76b-4b0e-4003-bb17-5297e9d00138/download)** · cited by 3  

> [PDF][PDF] Training eyes and training hands in the digital research with manuscriptsD Navarro Bonilla - 2021 - e-archivo.uc3m.esSuggests that incorporating actual writing practices, where students reproduce letters calligraphically, is a methodologically suggestive educational approach for improving competencies in manuscript analysis, which can be extended to digital paleography classrooms.

- **Dedicated Digital Paleography Course:** Explores the inclusion of a dedicated course, “Digital Paleography,” within a new bachelor’s degree in Management of Information and Digital Contents, demonstrating an institutional practice for teaching these topics to undergraduate students.
- **Evaluating Calligraphic Skills:** Discusses the challenge of properly designing a study program that not only integrates calligraphic skills into the paleography classroom but also establishes reliable evaluation criteria for the degree of calligraphic perfection, which is relevant for the practical aspects of HTR.
