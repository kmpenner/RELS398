PEDAGOGICAL CORE: THE DETERMINISTIC CONTRACT (READING INK, NOT LANGUAGE)

Frame the essential difference of Step 2: "Today's program is not an AI chatbot. It does not predict what sentence would make sense in English or French. It scans pixel strips from left to right and maps ink shapes directly to characters. This boundary is both its limitation and its greatest strength: it will struggle with tangled ligatures, but it will never hallucinate a plausible sentence that is not on the page. When it fails, it produces impossible character strings (loud errors), which tell on themselves instantly. On Friday, the LLM will do the exact opposite."

CRITICAL NOVICE TRAPS: CLIPPED DIACRITICS & OFF-BY-ONE ALIGNMENT

1. Truncated line envelopes: If a baseline polygon cuts off high breathing marks, accents, or ascenders, the model is trained on mutilated letters. It will learn that your scribe never writes diacritics, wrecking your error rate in Week 5.
2. Off-by-one text pairing: If layout analysis finds 25 lines but the student's transcription has 24, automatic alignment pairs every line after the discrepancy with the wrong image strip. Spot-check 3 lines by eye before training!

Classroom Diagnostics & Troubleshooting Triage

  • Off-by-One Cascading Shift: If line 3 was split into two baselines, line 4's text will bind to line 3's tail. Stop students immediately: delete extra baseline before importing text.
  • Polygon Ceiling Too Low: Scribes with prominent ascenders (b, d, l) or accents get decapitated. Check line cutouts: if letter heads are clipped, widen polygon height.
  • Validation Error Rising: If training curve drops then climbs, the model is overfitting (memorizing). Stop training at the lowest validation point.
  • Specialization Gap: A 100-million parameter general model (TrOCR/commercial OCR) will often lose to a tiny, domain-matched PyLaia model (14.6% vs 84.7% error). Reassure students that small matched models are superior.
  • Shared Credit Economy: Remind students not to trigger multiple training runs simultaneously; verify line alignment first to preserve Transkribus credit balance.
