Peer Assessment Rubric

  • Diplomatic Precision & Ground Truth (4 pts): The human transcription faithfully captures all graphemic features, line breaks, and scribal idiosyncrasies of the witness.
  • HTR & VLM Error Analysis (3 pts): The submission rigorously documents machine errors, correctly distinguishing baseline misalignment, ligature confusion, and text hallucination.
  • Methodological Reflection (3 pts): The 300–500 word post articulates why automated transcription requires human ground truth as an indispensable epistemic anchor.
