Workshop: Ground-Truth Diplomatic Transcription & HTR Auditing

Overview: In this workshop, you will produce a complete diplomatic transcription of your chosen full-page textual witness. You will run your folio through Transkribus HTR and an LLM/VLM transcription prompt, compare both machine outputs against your manual ground truth, and calculate your model's Character Error Rate (CER).

Deadlines: Comparison Post due Friday by 11:00 PM; Peer Reviews due Monday by 11:00 PM.
