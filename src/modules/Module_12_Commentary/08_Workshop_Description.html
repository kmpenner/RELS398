Workshop: Multi-Tiered Scholarly Commentary & Intertextual Linking

Overview: In this workshop, you will author five scholarly commentary notes for your primary witness passage spanning three distinct categories (philological, historical, intertextual). You will encode them using stand-off TEI markup, connect them to Linked Open Data authorities, and compare your manual analysis against an automated text-reuse discovery query.

Deadlines: Comparison Post due Friday by 11:00 PM; Peer Reviews due Monday by 11:00 PM.
