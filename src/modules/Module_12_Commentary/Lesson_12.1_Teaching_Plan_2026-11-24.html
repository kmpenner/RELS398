THE COMMENTARY DIRECTIVE: STRUCTURED STANDOFF ANNOTATION

A digital edition is not an uncritical photocopy. By categorizing notes into philological, historical, and intertextual domains within a standoff <standOff> block, students transform raw transcriptions into durable, searchable scholarly editions.

INVIOLABLE PEDAGOGICAL BOUNDARIES

1. Separate layers strictly: Keep the base transcription clean inside <body>; place all scholarly annotations inside <standOff>.
2. Stable anchors only: Every note must point to an explicit anchor via @target (e.g. target="#s1" or target="#w4"). Never rely on character offsets or line numbers.
3. The mandatory hash prefix: The hash symbol (#) is strictly required in all local @target values.
4. Three core categories: Every note must specify @type as philological, historical, or intertextual.
5. Tuesday ground truth baseline: Today's five hand-authored notes in commentary_manual.xml establish the gold standard used to audit Wednesday's authority matching and Friday's AI prompt generation.
