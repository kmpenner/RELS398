Submission Instructions

  1. Choose a passage of up to 5 sentences and add xml:id anchors to the words you will comment on.
  2. Manual: write three notes: one philological, one historical and one intertextual, each with its source.
  3. AI: ask an AI model to draft notes on the same passage. In a table in COMMENTARY.md, mark each AI note Accept, Revise, Reject or Flag, with a reason; check every fact it states.
  4. Encode the notes you keep as commentary_merged.xml: a <standOff> containing <note type="..." target="#id" resp="...">. Check it is well-formed.
  5. Submit COMMENTARY.md (table and a 150–250 word reflection at the end of the same file) and commentary_merged.xml.
