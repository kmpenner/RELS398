How to Use Your Interactive Lessons & Companion Media

Every class meeting is accompanied by a dedicated multi-modal learning package designed for mastery and accessibility:

  • Interactive Concept Lessons: Structured digital lessons featuring focused exposition, domain-specific illustrations, and inline multiple-choice self-checks with immediate diagnostic feedback.
  • Printable Offline Study Notes (PDF): Every lesson has an accompanying downloadable PDF formatted in the official 2026 StFX academic layout for distraction-free reading, highlighting, and offline study.
  • Companion Audio Overviews:
    • Brief Audio Overview (~2 mins): High-level orientation before diving into the readings.
    • Deep Dive Discussion (30–50 mins): Comprehensive, conversational exploration analyzing the underlying historical, philosophical, and computational debates.
  • Visual Infographic Aids: High-resolution landscape synthesis sheets capturing the workflows, schemas, and taxonomies of each lesson on a single page.
