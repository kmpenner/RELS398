Assigned Readings & Foundational Scholarship

Please study the following research papers on manuscript metadata and digital harmonization:

  • Hyvönen, Eero, et al. "Harmonizing and Publishing Heterogeneous Premodern Manuscript Metadata as Linked Open Data." Semantic Web Journal, 2023.
  • TEI Consortium. "Manuscript Description." In TEI P5: Guidelines for Electronic Text Encoding and Interchange, Chapter 10. TEI Consortium, 2024.
  • Romanello, Matteo, and Alban Gautier. "Automatic Metadata Extraction: Leveraging Large Language Models in Digital Humanities." Journal of Open Humanities Data, 2024.
  • Clarysse, Willy. "The Trismegistos Database of Ancient Texts and Manuscripts." Literary and Linguistic Computing, 2010.
