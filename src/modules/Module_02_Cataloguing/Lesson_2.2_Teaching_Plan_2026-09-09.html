xml-model Instruction for Projection

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<?xml-model href="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/msdesc/consolidated-tei-schema/master/msdesc.rng"
        type="application/xml"
        schematypens="http://relaxng.org/ns/structure/1.0"?>
<TEI xmlns="http://www.tei-c.org/ns/1.0">
  ...
</TEI>
<!-- Enrichment: strings become things -->
<repository ref="http://viaf.org/viaf/141312782">Biblioteca Apostolica Vaticana</repository>
<origPlace ref="https://pleiades.stoa.org/places/520998">Constantinople</origPlace>
<msItem n="1" corresp="urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0527.tlg001.opp-grc1:1.1-1.14">

Reading Validator Error Messages

  • "Element 'xyz' is not allowed here": wrong order or wrong parent container — check whether <titleStmt> precedes <publicationStmt> precedes <sourceDesc>.
  • "...must be terminated by the matching end-tag": a missing closing slash or an unclosed tag — scan for the open bracket without a partner.
  • "Invalid attribute scope for element handNote": a value outside a closed list — scope="main" must become scope="major" (permitted: sole / major / minor). Read the permitted values out of the schema; do not substitute a synonym.
  • The error that never fires: supportDesc/@material accepts paper, vellum, and even nonsense without complaint — its enumeration is documented but not enforced. Teach the gap rather than a vellum→parchment rule. Verified against msdesc.rng.
  • Workflow: click an error in the Problems panel to jump to the offending line; fix one at a time and watch the count drop — do not try to fix everything before re-saving.
  • Stage gate: the bottom-left Problems counter must read 0 Errors, 0 Warnings before enrichment or commit.
