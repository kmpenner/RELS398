Workshop: Codicological TEI Header & Authority Reconciliation

Overview: In this workshop, you will catalogue an assigned un-digitized Greek manuscript witness. You will query Trismegistos and Pinakes to identify authority records, construct a complete, validated TEI <msDesc> block, and audit an LLM-generated metadata record for hallucinated codicological data.
