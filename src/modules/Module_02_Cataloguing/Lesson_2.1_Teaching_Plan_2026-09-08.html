Minimal Starter Skeleton for Projection

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<TEI xmlns="http://www.tei-c.org/ns/1.0">
  <teiHeader>
    <fileDesc>
      <titleStmt>
        <title>Electronic Edition of [Your Document Title]</title>
        <author>[Author or Anonymous]</author>
        <editor>[Your Name]</editor>
      </titleStmt>
      <publicationStmt>
        <publisher>St. Francis Xavier University</publisher>
        <date when="2026">2026</date>
        <availability><licence target="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</licence></availability>
      </publicationStmt>
      <sourceDesc>
        <msDesc>
          <msIdentifier>
            <repository>[Holding Archive or Library]</repository>
            <idno>[Shelfmark / Call Number]</idno>
          </msIdentifier>
          <physDesc>
            <objectDesc><supportDesc material="paper"><p>Handmade rag paper.</p></supportDesc></objectDesc>
            <handDesc><handNote xml:id="h1">Primary scribal cursive script.</handNote></handDesc>
          </physDesc>
        </msDesc>
      </sourceDesc>
    </fileDesc>
  </teiHeader>
  <text><body><p>[Transcription to come in Module 4]</p></body></text>
</TEI>

Common Novice Pitfalls to Watch For

  • Unclosed or Mismatched Tags: Writing <title>...</author> or omitting the slash in </sourceDesc>.
  • Case Sensitivity: XML is strictly case sensitive: <teiHeader> is valid; <TeiHeader> or <TEIHEADER> will fail.
  • Missing Root Element: Forgetting the wrapping <TEI>...</TEI> container.
  • Accidental Word Quotes: If copying from notes, ensure straight quotes ("...") are used in attributes rather than curly smart quotes.
  • Saving in the Wrong Directory: Ensure witness.xml is saved inside ~/Documents/RELS398/ so Git detects it.
