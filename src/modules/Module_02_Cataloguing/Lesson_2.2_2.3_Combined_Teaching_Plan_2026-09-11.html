Reading Validator Error Messages

  • "Element 'xyz' is not allowed here" — wrong order or wrong parent. Check that <titleStmt> precedes <publicationStmt> precedes <sourceDesc>.
  • "...must be terminated by the matching end-tag" — a missing slash or unclosed tag. Scan for the open bracket without a partner.
  • "Invalid attribute scope for element handNote" — a value outside a closed list: scope="main" must become scope="major" (permitted: sole / major / minor). Read the permitted values out of the schema; do not substitute a synonym.
  • The error that never fires — supportDesc/@material accepts paper, vellum, and even nonsense without complaint; its enumeration is documented but not enforced. Do not teach a vellum→parchment rule. This is the bridge into 2.3: the schema checks only what it actually declares, and silence is not approval. Verified against msdesc.rng, 2026-09-10.
  • Workflow: click the error to jump to the line, fix one at a time, re-save, watch the count drop. Never batch-rewrite.
