THE SYNTHESIS OBJECTIVE: AUDITING AI AGAINST HUMAN BENCHMARKS

Generative models possess remarkable semantic breadth, resolving complex boundary errors and contextual nicknames that baffle deterministic sequence taggers. However, LLMs also introduce dangerous factual hallucinations, inventing convincing but synthetic authority URIs. By triangulating Manual Ground Truth, SpaCy, and LLM predictions, students evaluate where AI accelerates research and where human oversight is vital.

INVIOLABLE PEDAGOGICAL BOUNDARIES

1. Manual is the default, not the winner by fiat: In the merge, Tuesday's reading stands unless the SpaCy or AI reading is better and the student can show why (archival evidence, a verified registry record). A machine that catches a real error in the manual file is the method working; record it.
2. Strict schema and token alignment: AI-generated XML must pass TEI Relax NG schema validation and maintain 100% token alignment with the primary witness.
3. Active link verification: Every AI-generated @ref link must be tested against the live Wikidata or GeoNames registry to detect invented Q-numbers.
