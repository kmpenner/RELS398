The Master Diplomatic Prompt for Antigravity

PASTE INTO VS CODE ANTIGRAVITY WITH YOUR IMAGE ATTACHED:

You are an expert paleographer and scholarly text encoder.
Transcribe the attached historical document page into strict TEI diplomatic XML.

RULES:
1. DIPLOMATIC FIDELITY:
   - Transcribe exactly what is written on the page. Preserve original capitalization, punctuation, and archaic or irregular spelling.
   - Do NOT modernize or normalize words.
   - Do NOT expand abbreviations (e.g., keep "&c.", "ye", "Govr", "Janry" as written).
   - Do NOT correct obvious historical typos.

2. ALLOWED TEI ELEMENTS:
   - Wrap the transcription in <text><body><div>...</div></body></text>.
   - Use <pb facs="#[surface-id]"/> at the start of the page.
   - Use <lb/> (empty, self-closing) at every physical line break on the document.
   - For verse manuscripts, wrap stanzas in <lg> and lines in <l>. For prose, wrap paragraphs in <p>.
   - Use <unclear>word</unclear> if ink or handwriting is ambiguous.
   - Use <gap reason="damage" unit="chars" extent="3"/> (or similar) where physical damage obscures text.
   - Do NOT use <choice>, <orig>, <reg>, <abbr>, or <expan>.

3. OUTPUT FORMAT:
   - Return ONLY the clean, well-formed XML block. No conversational filler, no markdown explanations.

Cohort Specific Guidance & Live Traps

JAY · Emily Dickinson Recipe for Black Cake (MS Am 1118.7)

  • Numerals & Mixed Fractions: Watch how Gemini transcribes quantities (e.g. 19 Eggs vs 12 Eggs, and 1 1/2 Currants vs 1/2 Currants). Retain Gemini's raw choices for Friday!
  • Punctuation Dashes & Tildes: Dickinson uses hyphens, dashes, and tildes (2 Sugar-, Cloves ~ Mace-). Gemini often normalizes these into standard commas. Keep whatever Gemini proposes in transcription_ai.xml!
  • List vs. Narrative Layout: The leaf transitions from ingredient quantities to baking steps. Ensure both are enclosed in <p> with <lb/> rather than poetic <lg>.

  • Printed Typography (Long-s): Look closely at whether Gemini transcribed historical long-s (ſ) as modern 's' or preserved the archaic glyph. Both behaviors are valuable data points for Friday.

  • Colophon & Signatures: Watch for printed legal signatures (e.g. M. L. F. Duport) and printing location at the foot of the decree.

Classroom Diagnostics: Catching AI Errors

1. The "Helpful" Normalization Trap

Symptom: Gemini writes modern "January" instead of manuscript "Janry", or "the" instead of "ye".

Diagnosis: LLMs are autoregressive models trained to predict high-probability modern tokens. Reinforce: "Leave it in transcription_ai.xml!" Discovering that the AI smoothed archaic text is a prime finding for Friday's diff.

2. Raw Ampersand XML Parse Error

Symptom: Red squiggle in VS Code: 'The entity name must immediately follow the & in the entity reference'.

Diagnosis: Gemini transcribed a literal "&" or "&c." without XML escaping. In XML, bare ampersands are illegal syntax. Fix: change & to &amp; (e.g. &amp;c.).

3. Bare Text Inside <div>

Symptom: Schema error: 'Element text not allowed here'.

Diagnosis: In TEI, a <div> cannot hold character data directly; text must be wrapped inside <p> (prose) or <lg><l> (verse). Ensure paragraph wrappers surround all line breaks.

4. Unclosed Line Breaks

Symptom: Schema error: 'End tag does not match start tag'.

Diagnosis: Gemini emitted <lb> instead of self-closing milestone <lb/>. Remind students that line breaks in TEI are zero-width milestones, not containers.
