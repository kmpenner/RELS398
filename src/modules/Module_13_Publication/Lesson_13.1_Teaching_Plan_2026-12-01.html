THE EDITORIAL STUDIO DIRECTIVE: METHODOLOGICAL TRANSPARENCY

A scholarly edition is defined by its transparency. Every regularization, linguistic gloss, and commentary note must be justified by an explicit editorial policy declared in the introduction and encoded in the TEI header.

INVIOLABLE PEDAGOGICAL BOUNDARIES

1. Single-witness documentary fidelity: Do not collate phantom witnesses; focus on absolute fidelity to your unique primary artifact.
2. Complete archival coordinates: The introduction must state the exact repository name, geographic location, and archival call number/shelfmark.
3. Explicit normalization rules: Do not use vague generalizations; state the specific orthographic rules applied (e.g. 18th-century verb endings, folk phonetic spellings).
4. Transparent computational provenance: Formally disclose all AI drafting tools and document the human-in-the-loop verification protocol.
5. Multi-tiered open licensing: Declare explicit Creative Commons licensing (CC BY 4.0 for prose, CC0 for metadata, MIT for scripts).
