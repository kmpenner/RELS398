Peer Assessment Rubric

  • TEI Master Completeness & Validity (4 pts): The edition contains all required editorial layers, fully validates against the official TEI schema with 0 errors, and exhibits complete pointer resolution.
  • Multi-Modal Presentation Fidelity (3 pts): The compiled HTML player and PDF deliverables render cleanly, legibly, and responsively across desktop, print, and mobile viewports.
  • FAIR Compliance & Scholarly Reflection (3 pts): The portfolio provides explicit open licenses, comprehensive documentation, and a mature philological reflection on digital editorial methodology.
