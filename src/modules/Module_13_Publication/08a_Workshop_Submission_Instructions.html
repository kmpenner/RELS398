Submission Instructions

  1. Combine your header, facsimile, reconciled and normalized transcription, and the merged files from Modules 8–12 into one edition.xml. Layers you did not complete may be left out; say which in your statement.
  2. Check edition.xml is well-formed and every # pointer resolves.
  3. Produce edition.html with the stylesheet from class, and a PDF of it (the browser's Print to PDF is fine).
  4. Add a short LICENSE.md naming the licence for your text and for your images.
  5. Write a 200–300 word editorial statement in STATEMENT.md: what you edited, how, and what the digital methods showed you about your document.
  6. Submit the five files (or one ZIP of your repository).
