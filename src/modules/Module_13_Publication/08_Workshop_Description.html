Workshop: Final Edition Compilation & Multi-Modal Portfolio Assembly

Overview: In this capstone workshop, you will assemble, validate, and compile your semester-long scholarly edition portfolio. You will verify your full TEI document against our 12-gate quality assurance checklist, generate interactive web and print-ready PDF versions, and prepare an archival package ready for Zenodo and GitHub deposit.

Deadlines: Final Edition Submission due during December Exam Period; Peer Audit due by December 18.
