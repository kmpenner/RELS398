Submission Instructions

  1. Choose 10 consecutive words from your reconciled transcription.
  2. Manual: for each word, record its dictionary lemma and its part of speech (UPOS tag) in a table in LINGUISTIC_AUDIT.md.
  3. spaCy and AI: run the same 10 words through spaCy (the in-class script) and through an AI model in VS Code; add their lemma and POS to the same table, one column each.
  4. Merge: in a final column, mark which reading you keep for each word (M, N or A) and why, in a few words. Manual is the default, not the winner by fiat.
  5. Encode the merged result as linguistic_merged.xml using <w lemma="..." pos="..." resp="...">token</w>, and check it is well-formed.
  6. Submit LINGUISTIC_AUDIT.md (table plus a 150–250 word reflection at the end of the same file: which kinds of words did the machines get wrong?) and linguistic_merged.xml.
