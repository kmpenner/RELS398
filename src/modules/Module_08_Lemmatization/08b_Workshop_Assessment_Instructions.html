Peer Assessment Rubric

  • Manual Ground Truth Precision (4 pts): The 20-word manual parse accurately identifies dictionary lemmas and correct inflectional morphology without grammatical errors.
  • TEI Linguistic Markup Validity (3 pts): The XML cleanly wraps tokens in <w> tags with valid lemma and pos attributes conforming to TEI P5 guidelines.
  • Comparative Error Analysis (3 pts): The 300–500 word analysis insightfully discusses why automated engines struggle with homographs, dialectal forms, or rare irregular conjugations.
