THE SYNTHESIS OBJECTIVE: AUDITING AI AGAINST HUMAN BENCHMARKS

Generative models possess remarkable contextual flexibility, often interpreting archaic spellings that break deterministic NLP taggers. However, LLMs also introduce non-deterministic hallucinations, invent citation forms, and succumb to prompt drift. By conducting a rigorous three-way comparative audit (Manual Ground Truth vs. SpaCy/TreeTagger vs. LLM Agent), students discover exactly where AI accelerates scholarship and where human editorial judgment remains irreplaceable.

INVIOLABLE PEDAGOGICAL BOUNDARIES

3. Diagnostic documentation over blind acceptance: Students must not simply accept AI output; every disagreement must be analyzed and recorded in LINGUISTIC_AUDIT.md.
