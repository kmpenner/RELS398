THE LEMMATIZATION OBJECTIVE: GROUND TRUTH BEFORE AUTOMATION

A computer cannot understand that treaties, treaty, and treaty's belong to the same word family, or whether left is a noun or a verb. Automated taggers make frequent mistakes on historical documents. Therefore, students must establish an authoritatively verified manual ground truth by hand before testing computational pipelines on Wednesday and Friday.

INVIOLABLE PEDAGOGICAL BOUNDARIES

1. Ground truth comes first: Today's 20-word manual parse serves as the immutable gold-standard benchmark against which Wednesday's deterministic tagger (TreeTagger/SpaCy) and Friday's AI model will be scored.
2. Standard citation lexica: Every lemma must be verified in an authoritative dictionary (OED for English, TLFi for French). Never invent modern colloquial lemmas or leave forms unverified.
3. Standard UPOS tags: Use the 17 Universal Dependencies UPOS tags (NOUN, VERB, ADJ, DET, PRON, ADP, etc.) rather than ad-hoc labels.
4. Nesting rule: When combining spelling regularizations from Week 6 with linguistic markup, <choice> always nests inside <w>, never the reverse.
5. Assignment alignment: Work produced today directly builds the ground-truth baseline for Assignment: Module 8 (Linguistic Markup), due Friday, October 30, at 11:00 PM.
