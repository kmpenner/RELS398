THE ALIGNMENT DIRECTIVE: STANDOFF ARCHITECTURE FOR BILINGUAL EDITIONS

A digital translation is not an inert paraphrase. By encoding sentence correspondences inside a standoff <linkGrp> and applying the Leipzig Glossing Rules, students make every translation decision explicit, verifiable, and computationally accessible.

INVIOLABLE PEDAGOGICAL BOUNDARIES

1. Use VS Code split editor: Keep source text on the left pane and translation on the right pane (View > Editor Layout > Two Columns) to prevent cognitive overload.
2. Standoff linking only: Never paste modern translations directly into the historical manuscript XML tree; preserve both files and link them via <linkGrp>.
3. The hash mark prefix: Every ID inside a @target attribute must be preceded by a hash mark (target="#s1 #t1").
4. Strict Leipzig Rule 2: Use hyphens (-) only for visible morpheme cuts; use periods (.) for fused grammatical categories (e.g. DEF.SG or 3SG.PST).
5. Tuesday ground truth baseline: Today's hand-crafted files (alignment_manual.xml and gloss_manual.xml) establish the gold standard used to audit Wednesday's automated tools and Friday's AI output.

  • Andromeda (1752 Peace and Friendship Treaty): Focus on aligning formal colonial treaty articles; gloss modal verbs of obligation (shall be renewed) and passive legal agreements.
