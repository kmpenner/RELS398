Module 11: Text Alignment & Interlinear Glossing — Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, students will be able to:

  • Construct four-line Interlinear Glossed Text (IGT) adhering strictly to the international Leipzig Glossing Rules, distinguishing morpheme boundaries from grammatical category labels.
  • Encode sentence-level and token-level parallel alignments in TEI P5 using stand-off <linkGrp> and <link> architecture.
  • Analyze the operational principles of sentence and word alignment algorithms, contrasting length-based heuristics (Gale-Church) with statistical translation models (IBM Models 1–5, fast_align).
  • Utilize specialized digital humanities alignment platforms (Ugarit, Alpheios Alignment Editor) to produce verified parallel corpora.
  • Audit and evaluate neural LLM translation alignments, identifying failure modes such as non-isomorphic syntactic distortion, hallucinated morphemes, and idiom mistranslation.
