THE DIRECTIVE: TRUST THE TOKENIZATION ONLY AFTER PROVING IT

Every link points at an xml:id, so an AI tokenization that changed one letter or repeated one id corrupts the whole alignment. Ace first proves the text unchanged, then link at three levels (s for every sentence, seg for phrases, w for one or two pairs), and only then reconcile the AI’s links with their own.

INVIOLABLE PEDAGOGICAL BOUNDARIES

1. Strict negative constraints: The tokenization prompt forbids changing, modernizing or correcting any letter, accent or space, and forbids adding, dropping or moving anything (including <choice>, <lb/>, <note>).
2. Text diff before linking: Plain text of old and tokenized files must match (diff -w prints nothing); xmllint --noout passes, so no duplicate ids.
3. Manual before AI: Students save alignment_manual.xml before they see the AI’s links; the AI file is saved untouched as alignment_ai.xml.
4. Transparent TEI attribution: Record the Gemini tokenization and the AI links, with what was adopted and rejected, in <revisionDesc>; @resp on each <linkGrp>.
5. Module 11 assignment milestone: Every file passes xmllint --noout and the viewer’s status line shows no missing targets before committing.
