THE SYNTHESIS DIRECTIVE: RECONCILING THREE METHODS

A digital scholarly edition combines computational efficiency with human philological rigor. By comparing manual baseline, deterministic formulas, and AI models side by side, students synthesize a final reconciled edition that leverages technology without compromising historical truth.

INVIOLABLE PEDAGOGICAL BOUNDARIES

1. Strict negative constraints: Prompting must forbid the AI from modernizing historical spelling, hallucinating classical roots, or using hyphens for fused grammatical endings.
2. Audit Line 1 by eye: Verify Line 1 letter-by-letter against the digital facsimile to guarantee that authentic historical spelling was not smoothed over.
3. Enforce Leipzig Rule 2: Ensure periods (.) are used for portmanteaux in Line 3, replacing any invalid AI hyphens.
4. Transparent TEI attribution: Record AI assistance transparently inside <revisionDesc> in the TEI header.
5. Module 11 assignment milestone: Reconciled files must pass xmllint --noout with zero errors before staging and committing.
