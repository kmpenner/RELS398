Submission Instructions

  1. Choose 5 consecutive sentences and a modern translation (published, or your own).
  2. Manual and AI: pair each source sentence with its translation by hand, then ask an AI model to do the same; record both in a table in ALIGNMENT.md, marking where they differ and which you keep.
  3. Encode the merged alignment as alignment_merged.xml: xml:id="s1"… on source sentences, xml:id="t1"… on translation sentences, and a <linkGrp type="translation-alignment"> of <link target="#s1 #t1"/> elements. Check it is well-formed.
  4. Write a four-line Leipzig gloss for one sentence in ALIGNMENT.md.
  5. Submit ALIGNMENT.md (table, gloss, and a 150–250 word reflection at the end of the same file) and alignment_merged.xml.
