OPERATIONAL TARGET & CORE DIRECTIVE:
Each student runs hunalign (Gale-Church length statistics plus an optional dictionary) inside the InterText desktop editor, exports the raw alignment before correcting it, calculates Precision and Recall against their Tuesday manual ground truth, and only then corrects the cascading domino errors in InterText. Students install InterText before class (announced at the end of 11.1); the Mac build is unsigned, so Mac users must allow it in Privacy & Security. Alpheios and Ugarit are manual word-level aligners and are not used here.

Walk-in Music (plays as students arrive, before minute 0)

  • Steve Reich, “Clapping Music” (1972). Two players clap the same 12-beat pattern; one shifts ahead a beat at a time, so the parts drift out of step and then realign.
  • Pedagogical connection: A length-based aligner keeps two texts in step by counting. Miss one sentence boundary and every later pair slips by one, the domino effect on slide 10; the human editor catches the slip.
  • Springboard at minute 0: “Yesterday you kept the two texts in step by hand. Today we let a counting rule do it, and watch for the moment it falls a beat behind.”

THE COMPARATIVE DIRECTIVE: AUDITING ALGORITHMIC QUALITY

Scholarly digital humanities is not about blindly trusting software. By measuring an algorithm's proposed links against Tuesday's human baseline, students learn to calculate precision and recall, diagnose the cascading domino effect, and supervise automated tools with philological rigor.

INVIOLABLE PEDAGOGICAL BOUNDARIES

1. Intuitive length explanation: Explain Gale-Church through character counts and visual ratios, avoiding intimidating calculus or probability notation.
2. Score before you fix: Students export hunalign’s raw output before touching it. Precision and recall measure the aligner, not the student’s corrections.
3. Spotting the domino effect: Emphasize that a single missed sentence break cascades into following pairs; show students how to identify and reset anchor points.
4. The audit obligation: Students must compare the algorithmic output against their Tuesday file in a two-column split editor and record discrepancy counts.
5. Save output cleanly: Exported and verified markup must be saved as alignment_deterministic.xml in the project root.
