Assigned Readings & Foundational Scholarship

Please study the following key literature on text alignment, linguistic glossing, and parallel digital editions:

  • Bickel, Balthasar, Bernard Comrie, and Martin Haspelmath. "The Leipzig Glossing Rules: Conventions for Multilingual Interlinear Glosses." Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology & Department of Linguistics, University of Leipzig, 2015.
  • TEI Consortium. "Chapter 16: Linking, Segmentation, and Alignment." TEI P5 Guidelines, Version 4.8.0, 2024. Focus on § 16.1.3 (Blocks of Correspondence: linkGrp).
  • Tiedemann, Jörg. "Parallel Data Extraction." Bitext Alignment, Morgan & Claypool Publishers, 2011.
  • Baumgardt, Sonja. "Digital Interlinear Editions: Challenges and Perspectives for Ancient and Medieval Texts." Journal of Digital Humanities, 2020.
