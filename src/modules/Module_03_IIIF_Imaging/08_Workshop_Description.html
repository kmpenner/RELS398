Assignment: Anchoring Your Page to Its Image

Overview: Your TEI header describes your document. Nothing in your file yet points at a picture of it. In this assignment you attach the image: you establish which image you are entitled to use, measure it, write a TEI <facsimile> that maps regions of that image, and link those regions to your text.

This is the Module 3 version of the three-step cycle. You will get your coordinates twice — once by eye and once from a tool that measures instead of guessing — and the difference between the two is part of what you submit.

A note on IIIF. Whether your holding institution publishes IIIF is outside your control and differs for each of your four documents. So the manifest task below is written as a choice: if your repository serves IIIF, examine what it actually gives you; if it does not, that is a finding about the state of your document's digitization, and you write it up as one. Neither path is worth more marks.

Deliverables: a validated TEI <facsimile> block, an image provenance record, your coordinate comparison, and a 300–500 word reflection. Submit them to the assignment.

Deadlines: the submission and assessment dates shown in this assignment's Moodle settings.
