Workshop: Multi-Branch Textual Scriptorium

Overview: In this workshop, you will simulate a collaborative scholarly scriptorium using Git. You will manage two divergent scribal transcriptions (representing Codex A and Codex B) on separate Git branches, normalize the Greek Unicode encodings to NFC, merge the branches while resolving textual conflicts, and submit your Git log alongside a short methodological reflection.

Deadlines:

  • Peer Reviews (2 assigned peers): Following Monday by 11:00 PM Atlantic
