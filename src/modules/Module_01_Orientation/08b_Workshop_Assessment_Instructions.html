Peer Assessment Rubric (10 pts)

For each criterion, state what earns credit and name one specific improvement. "Looks good" earns nothing.

  • Witness Identification & Checks (4 pts): The README identifies the institution, the shelfmark or reference in the institution's own form, the object type, the single page, and the image URL. All five checks are answered with evidence, such as a catalogue link, not just asserted. Try the catalogue link: an identifier that cannot be traced to a record earns no credit on this criterion. A failed check that is honestly recorded earns full credit.
  • Git Practice (3 pts): The log shows at least three commits after the first. Each commit contains one logical change, and each message says specifically what changed. Deduct for a single catch-all commit, or for messages like "update" or "fix".
  • Rights & FAIR (3 pts): The submission separates object rights from image licence and quotes the terms. The FAIR statement names a concrete decision for each of the four principles, plus a difficulty specific to this document. Generic definitions of FAIR earn at most 1 point.

Marking guide: 9–10 exceptional; 7–8 solid and verifiable; 5–6 complete but asserted rather than evidenced; below 5 means a criterion is unaddressed.
