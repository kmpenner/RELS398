09:36 – 09:48
(12 mins)		 2. First-Look Lecture: Editions & FAIR
Teach from Scratch (Whiteboard) 		• What is a Digital Edition? Explain that ancient manuscripts are dispersed, fragile, and individually variable. Print editions hide the evidence in footnotes; digital editions make the evidence computable.
• Introduce FAIR (Plain English): Findable (permanent IDs), Accessible (open web/IIIF), Interoperable (standard TEI XML), Reusable (documented in Git).
• Draw the 8-Stage Roadmap: Catalogue → IIIF → HTR Transcription → Reconciliation → Collation → Linguistic Markup → Alignment → Web Publication.
09:48 – 09:56
(8 mins)		 3. Instructor Live Modeling

• Demonstrate the Full-Page Concept: Point out ruling lines, margins, folio numbers, and ink condition. Show why selecting a full page preserves physical context.
• Explain the 5 Checks: 1) High-res image; 2) Catalog shelfmark; 3) Known text; 4) Legible hand (no unreadable palimpsests!); 5) Benchmark edition exists.
