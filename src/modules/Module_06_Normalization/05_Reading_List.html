Assigned Readings & Foundational Scholarship

Please study the following chapters and methodological frameworks on primary source representation and variant taxonomy:

  • TEI Consortium. "Chapter 11: Representation of Primary Sources." TEI P5: Guidelines for Electronic Text Encoding and Interchange, Version 4.8.0, 2024. Focus on § 11.3.1.2 (Alteration) and § 11.3.1.4 (Regularization).
  • Griffitts, Troy. "Digital Editing of Biblical Texts." Manuscript Cultures: Mapping Textual Landscapes, § 4.6.1 (Orthographic Regularization and Collation Baselines).
  • Sabel, Helena. "The Encoding of Variant Taxonomies in TEI: Distinguishing Error from Linguistic Diversity." Journal of the Text Encoding Initiative, Issue 12, 2019.
