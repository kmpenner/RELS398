Module 06: Orthographic Normalization — Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, students will be able to:

  • Distinguish between meaningful textual variation and phonological or orthographic drift (such as Greek iotacism, vowel mergers, and early modern English spelling fluidity).
  • Encode diplomatic readings and normalized editorial regularizations using TEI <choice>, <orig>, and <reg> markup.
  • Construct and execute a deterministic Python script utilizing regular expressions (re.sub) to automate rule-based orthographic normalization.
  • Audit AI-generated normalization outputs against a manual ground truth to identify and mitigate the risks of "hypercorrection" and erasure of dialectal evidence.
