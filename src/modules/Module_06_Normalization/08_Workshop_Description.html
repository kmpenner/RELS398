Workshop: Comparative Orthographic Normalization (Manual vs. Script vs. LLM)

Overview: In this workshop, you will apply three successive normalization methods to a 20-line passage of your primary manuscript witness. You will compare manual TEI <choice><orig>...<reg>...</choice> encoding against a deterministic Python regex script and a prompt-engineered LLM normalization, documenting every disagreement and evaluating hypercorrection errors.

Deadlines: Comparison Post due Friday by 11:00 PM; Peer Reviews due Monday by 11:00 PM.
