# Moodle Course Announcement Draft

**Subject:** Welcome Back from Study Break: Updated Schedule & Week 7 (Rendering & Digital Publication)

Dear Class,

I hope you have had a restful Fall Study Break! As we head into the second half of the term, classes resume on Tuesday, October 20 at 8:30 AM in MULH 3032.

Before the break, each of you brought your primary textual witness through diplomatic transcription, double-keying reconciliation, and orthographic normalization. Because our class projects are focused on unique historical witnesses (Ace’s 1791 French Revolutionary decree, Olyvia’s Maud Lewis correspondence, and Andromeda’s 1752 Peace and Friendship Treaty), none of our projects requires reconstructing a lost archetype from multiple manuscript copies. Consequently, we are adjusting the second half of our schedule: we are setting aside multi-witness collation and the critical apparatus in favour of practical publication and named-entity markup.

### What Changes in the Schedule:
1. **Week 7 (Oct 20–23): Rendering & Digital Publication I**
   We will take the normalized TEI XML you completed in Week 6 and turn it into editions that real people can read. We will explore hand-authored HTML and CSS rules (Tuesday), automated transformation with XSLT and in-browser rendering with CETEIcean (Wednesday), and auditing AI-generated stylesheets (Friday).
2. **Week 8 (Oct 27–30): Lemmatization & Morphological Tagging**
   We move to linguistic analysis and dictionary headwords, adapted to our English and French documents. (Linguistic Markup assignment due Friday, October 30).
3. **Week 9 (Nov 3–6): Named Entities & Linked Open Data**
   We will identify and encode people, places, organizations, and historical dates (`<persName>`, `<placeName>`, `<date>`) and link them to authority files. (Named-Entity Markup assignment due Friday, November 6).
4. **Grading Breakdown:**
   The 5% grade previously allocated to "Textual Variants & Collation" is reallocated to the **Named-Entity Markup Assignment (5%)**. Week 7 Rendering feeds directly into your final Print-Ready Publication component.
